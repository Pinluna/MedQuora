    PPB MODEL EXAM PRACTICE

    Board exam simulations

    Each paper is a 60-question, 60-minute simulation. Paper 1 and Paper 2 banks are strictly separated by topic. Sessions use only unseen questions; reset a paper when fewer than 60 remain. These are practice simulations—not official papers or a past-year archive.

    The 3,870-question bank combines Pharmacy Quiz Challenge (MIT), MedMCQA (Apache-2.0), RxQA (CC BY 4.0), DurgiPedia (CC BY 4.0), Eka NFI (MIT), OpenALG (CC BY 4.0), OpenExamPrep, PharmaProLearn, MedQuora original questions, the local PPB Board Mock Series volumes, and the PPB Stage Two Paper II model paper. Every item carries its source, license, and region tag; Scribd answers without a published key are labelled editorial best-evidence. Read the open-source licenses. Content is not officially endorsed or clinically reviewed.
