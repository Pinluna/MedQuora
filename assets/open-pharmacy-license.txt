Additional question sources
---------------------------
MedMCQA (https://huggingface.co/datasets/openlifescienceai/medmcqa) — question
data licensed under Apache-2.0. Each question keeps its source answer key and
explanation. Region tag: IN.

RxQA (https://github.com/Google-Health/rxqa) — pharmacist-validated annotations
licensed CC BY 4.0. Region tag: US.

DurgiPedia (https://durgical.com/en/practice/questions.json) — questions and
teaching points licensed CC BY 4.0. Region tag: GLOBAL.

Eka NFI MCQA (https://huggingface.co/datasets/ekacare/Eka_NFI_MCQA) — licensed
MIT. Region tag: IN.

OpenALG chemistry ancillary materials
(https://oer.galileo.usg.edu/chemistry-ancillary/18) — licensed CC BY 4.0.
Region tag: US.

OpenExamPrep, PharmaProLearn, and the PPB Stage Two Paper II model paper remain
attributed in each question's `source` and `sourceUrl` fields. Questions without
a published answer key are marked with editorial best-evidence in `answerBasis`.
All questions are for revision only and are not official examination material
or clinical guidance.
